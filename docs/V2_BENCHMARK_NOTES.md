# V2 — Benchmark Notes

## Purpose
Capture useful external patterns for Ainos V2 without copying another firm's identity or introducing unsupported claims.

## PJT Partners
Reference: https://www.pjtpartners.com/

What is useful for Ainos:
- A strong declarative opening statement instead of UI-heavy framing
- Clear capability architecture with substantial whitespace
- Institutional tone with minimal decorative interface elements
- Senior-team credibility integrated into the narrative

What not to copy:
- Large-bank scale cues, transaction claims or metrics that Ainos cannot substantiate

## General Atlantic
Reference: https://www.generalatlantic.com/

What is useful for Ainos:
- Strong narrative progression from positioning to approach to strategies / capabilities
- Large typography carrying hierarchy
- Selective use of imagery and sectional contrast instead of repeated cards
- Geography presented as a meaningful global-reach module
- Editorial content architecture that can later support insights/news

What not to copy:
- AUM / capital / office statistics, portfolio scale, or institutional investment claims

## Kearney
Reference: https://www.kearney.com/

What is useful for Ainos:
- Service architecture that remains understandable even as content depth grows
- Strong content/navigation system
- Ability to support an insights layer without forcing it into the primary homepage story

What not to copy:
- Mega-menu complexity or enterprise-consulting breadth inappropriate for a small senior team

## Synthesis for Ainos
The best V2 direction is not to look like a large consulting firm or investment bank. Ainos should combine:
- PJT-like restraint and seniority
- General Atlantic-like editorial storytelling and global visual confidence
- Kearney-like content extensibility

while staying visibly smaller, more entrepreneurial, more direct and more execution-oriented.
