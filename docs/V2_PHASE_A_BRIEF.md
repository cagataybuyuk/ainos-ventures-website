# V2 Phase A — Prototype Brief

## Goal
Produce a materially more premium, editorial and distinctive homepage direction while preserving the approved Ainos identity and content.

## Surfaces in scope
- Navigation
- Hero
- Current Focus
- Team
- Markets & Reach

## Art-direction rules
- Warm ivory / charcoal remains the core palette
- Keep the official Ainos monogram
- Use typography and whitespace as primary hierarchy tools
- Reduce rounded containers and pill density
- Use circles / linework as restrained signature geometry
- Avoid stock imagery and generic consulting visuals
- Avoid SaaS dashboard cues
- Maintain a small-senior-team feel rather than mimicking a large bank

## Prototype acceptance criteria
- The hero no longer reads like a product dashboard/card
- Sections have visibly different information structures
- Current Focus has more narrative hierarchy than four equal cards
- Team feels portrait-led and senior
- Markets & Reach feels institutional rather than tag-like
- Motion concepts are subtle and optional, not required for comprehension
- Mobile is designed as its own composition, not just stacked desktop
- No approved copy or public facts are substantively changed
